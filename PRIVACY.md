# Escape Keyboard — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

Escape Keyboard is an on-screen keyboard (input method) for Android.
This policy explains what happens to the information you type.

## Summary

Escape Keyboard does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your
personal data. The app has no internet permission, so it technically cannot
send anything you type over the network.

## What the keyboard processes

- **Text you type.** Keystrokes are processed only on your device to insert
  text into the app you are using and, if word suggestions are enabled, to
  suggest words. Nothing is transmitted.
- **Learned words.** If suggestions are on, words you type often may be saved
  in a local dictionary on your device to improve suggestions. You can clear
  them by clearing the app's data in Android settings.
- **Android user dictionary.** With your device's permission system the
  keyboard can read and add words to the Android personal dictionary, which
  stays on your device.
- **Clipboard history.** If clipboard history is on (Settings, Clipboard),
  text you copy while Escape Keyboard is your active keyboard is saved in the
  app's private storage on your device so you can paste it again. This storage
  is excluded from Android backup. Copies that apps mark as sensitive (for
  example passwords from a password manager) are not saved. You can turn the
  history off, delete entries or clear it from the clipboard window.
- **Settings.** Your keyboard settings are stored on your device. If Android
  backup is enabled for your Google account, Android may include these
  settings (not your typed text) in your device backup.
- **Voice input.** If you use the voice key, the keyboard hands over to the
  speech recognition service installed on your device (for example Google).
  That service's own privacy policy applies; Escape Keyboard does not receive
  the audio.

## What the keyboard does not do

- No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- No accounts, no sign-in.
- No access to contacts, location, camera or files.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from
anyone.
